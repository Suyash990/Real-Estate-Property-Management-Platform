About NestKey

A simple platform that brings property owners and tenants together, so renting feels clear, fair and easy.

Our mission

Managing a rented property usually means paper receipts, phone calls and lost messages. NestKey puts listings, rent tracking and maintenance requests in one place, so owners save time and tenants always know where things stand.

Our vision

We want every property owner in cities like Mathura, Agra and Noida to manage their homes without spreadsheets, and every tenant to find and rent a home with confidence.

What NestKey offers

For owners

List properties with photos and prices, see which are available, occupied or under repair.

For tenants

Search homes by city or locality, view full details and pay rent from one dashboard.

For maintenance

Report a problem in a minute and track the request until it is fixed.

How it works

  1. SearchFind a property by city, locality or PIN code.
  2. ExploreOpen a property to see photos, amenities and price.
  3. ConnectSend an enquiry to the owner from the details page.
  4. ManageTrack rent and maintenance from your dashboard.
